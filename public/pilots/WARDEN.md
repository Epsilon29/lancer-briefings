Sara "WARDEN" Novaks was stationed as a junior administrator on Umara as part of the Union's few administrative liasion offices in the Karrakin Trade Baronies. She graduated top of her class from the Commissariat for Civic Governance, scoring perfect marks on her Bureaucratic Efficiency and Governance Aptitude Test. After her tour on Umara, she will be well on her way to becoming a full-fledged Union Administrator. She may even get a posting in the core systems, assuming she doesn't piss off the wrong people...

Further background checks reveals membership in the Verdant Social Arc (VSA), a social-democratic left coalition that emerged as an anti-colonial front against SecComm’s imperialism.

She stands with a small frame, wearing the standard Union Administrator attire—an immaculate suit in dark Union Gray. Her hair is styled neatly in two buns at the back of her head. A silver pip on her collar signifies her rank. Her eyes are keen and calculating, always one step ahead, and her posture is perfect. She would make for a good soldier if she wasn't a better administrator. Though her size might suggest fragility, her presence fills any room she enters, commanding respect and attention—or the ire of anyone who dares oppose her.
